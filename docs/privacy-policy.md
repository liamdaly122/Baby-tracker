**Last updated: [date]**

Mission Control is made by [developer name or company] ("we"). This page explains what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and what it never does.

## The short version

- Everything you put into Mission Control stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
- We don't run any servers. There are no accounts, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking.
- We never see what you put into the app, so we can't sell it, share it or lose it.
- Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it yourself: a call, a text, directions or a shared birth plan.

## What the app keeps, and where

All of this is stored only on your devices.

**About the pregnancy and the plan.** Kept on your iPhone:

- the due date;
- her name, hospital number and anything else you add for the call;
- her maternity unit and a backup unit: their names, numbers, address and journey time;
- the people you want to call, with their names, numbers and roles;
- her birth plan;
- after the birth, what happened (for example, if the baby needed extra care).

This is included in your iPhone's backup, if you have backups turned on, so it comes back if you restore your phone. Apple encrypts iCloud backups. We don't use iCloud Drive or any other iCloud storage for it.

**During labour.** The times of each contraction, any red flags you switch on (such as her waters breaking), and notes of calls you make. These are kept on your iPhone and your Apple Watch. The labour log is not included in your iPhone's backup.

**On your Apple Watch.** A copy of what the watch needs for labour: her name, the due date, the unit's name and numbers, the rules for when to call, and the labour log. Your iPhone sends it to your watch over Apple's encrypted connection between them. It doesn't go through us.

**Small settings.** A random code that lets your phone and watch tell their taps apart, and a few switches, such as whether you've already read the opening notice.

## What the app never does

- It never sends your information to us or anyone else. The app itself never connects to the internet.
- It doesn't use Apple Health, your location, your microphone or your camera.
- It doesn't read your contacts. When you choose "Choose from Contacts", iOS shows you your contacts, and the app copies only the name and number you pick.
- It has no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code from anyone else.

## When information leaves your phone

Only when you do it:

- **Calls and texts.** These go through your phone's normal calling and Messages. The app gives your phone the number; it doesn't listen to or record calls, or see your messages.
- **Directions.** "Directions" opens Apple Maps with the unit's address, under Apple's privacy terms.
- **Links.** Links to NHS pages and to support charities open in your web browser, like any other link.
- **Sharing.** When you share the birth plan or a message after the birth, you choose the app and the person it goes to. After that, it's handled by that app (for example Messages or WhatsApp) under its own privacy terms.
- **Alerts.** The app sets up alerts on your phone and watch to tell you when it's time to call. They're created on the device and never go through a server. They can show on your lock screen. They say what to do, such as "Time to call your maternity unit", and never include her name, her hospital number or the unit's name. You can change how they show in Settings, Notifications, Mission Control.

## Information Apple may share with us

If you've agreed in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and overall usage numbers that don't identify you. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

If you're testing a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us your name and email address (if we invited you by email), your device model, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you send. We use these only to fix the app, keep them for [period], and delete them when testing ends. [To be set up before testing starts.]

## Other people's information

Most of what's in the app is about her, and some is about the people on your call list. Please make sure she's happy with what you keep, and that the people on your list are happy to be on it. She can ask you to change or delete anything.

## Deleting your information

To delete everything without deleting the app, go to Your data on the home screen and tap Delete everything. It removes everything listed above from your iPhone, and from your Apple Watch as soon as it's connected, and asks you twice first. It doesn't delete a labour that's still running. Deleting the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch also deletes everything it keeps there. Copies in your backups stay until you delete those backups.

Because we never receive your information, we can't see, change or delete it for you. Everything is in your hands.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your phone, we'll tell you in the app first.
