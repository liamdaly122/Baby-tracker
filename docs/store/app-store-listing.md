## Description (4,000 at most)

When labour starts, you're the one holding the phone. Mission Control helps you get ready in the weeks before, then keeps you calm and useful on the day.

BEFORE THE BIRTH
- Count down to the due date.
- Save her maternity unit's numbers, checked against her notes, and a backup unit.
- Keep the people to call: who's driving, who has the children, who to tell.
- Write her birth plan together, so you can speak up for what she wants.
- Run a practice labour, so the real one isn't the first time.

ON THE DAY
- Lift the cover and press LAUNCH.
- Tap once when a contraction starts and again when it stops, on your iPhone or Apple Watch.
- See the NHS advice on when to phone her maternity unit, with the numbers behind it and a link to the NHS page.
- Switch on a red flag, such as her waters breaking or the baby moving less, to see what the NHS says to do.
- Call the unit in one tap, with a script to read out and "Can't get through?" options. 999 is always one tap away.
- Big buttons and a dark screen, made for one hand at 3am.

YOUR INFORMATION STAYS WITH YOU
No accounts, no adverts, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

PLEASE NOTE
Mission Control is based on NHS guidance. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the NHS. It doesn't diagnose anything or tell you what stage of labour she's in. Her midwife's advice always comes first, and you can call her maternity unit any time you're worried. In an emergency, call 999.
