# TestFlight: what testers see

Draft, 2 November 2026. These go in App Store Connect's "Test Information", and the notice goes to each tester before they're invited (review item PRT-05). Like the listing, everything in this folder is checked by `Scripts/claims-lint.py`. The parts in square brackets need filling in.

## Beta app description

Mission Control is an iPhone and Apple Watch app for birth partners in the UK. It times contractions, shows the NHS advice on when to phone her maternity unit, and keeps the unit's numbers, a call script and her birth plan to hand.

This is an early test version. Please try the practice run, set up a pretend pregnancy, and tell us what's confusing, slow or wrong.

## What to test

- Set up the mission profile, a hospital and the people to call, with made-up names and numbers.
- Run the practice run from the home screen. It never dials anyone and sends no alerts.
- Lift the cover and press LAUNCH, then time a few pretend contractions on the phone and the watch.
- Try "Your data", and Delete everything when you've finished.
- Tell us anything that's hard to read in the dark, or hard to tap with one hand.

## The tester notice

Please read this before you join the test.

**This is a test version, not the finished app.** Its advice hasn't yet been checked by a midwife. Please don't rely on it in a real labour. Follow her midwife's advice, and call her maternity unit as you normally would. In an emergency, call 999.

**Use made-up details.** Try it with pretend names and numbers. The practice run never dials anyone.

**What we see.** The app keeps everything on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and never sends it to us. Through TestFlight, Apple gives us:

- your name and email address, if we invited you by email;
- your device model and iOS version;
- crash reports;
- any feedback and screenshots you choose to send.

We use these only to improve the app, keep them until [date or period], and then delete them.

**Telling us about a problem.** Send feedback from the TestFlight app, or take a screenshot in Mission Control and choose Share Beta Feedback. If anything could put someone at risk (advice that seems wrong, a call that rings the wrong number, an alert that doesn't arrive), please also email [contact email] with "Safety" in the subject. We'll look at it within one working day.

**Leaving the test.** Delete the app, and leave the test in the TestFlight app. Ask us at [contact email] and we'll delete what Apple has shared with us about you.

By joining, you agree to the above.
