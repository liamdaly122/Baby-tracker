## Threshold decisions (thresholds.json)

The defaults and safe bounds for the go/no-go engine live in `Sources/MissionContent/Content/{uk,us}/thresholds.json`, with a compiled-in copy in `ThresholdTable.builtIn(for:)` that a test keeps identical.

| # | Decision | Reason | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | UK `adviceDurationSec` defaults to 0, meaning "no duration requirement". The 30 to 90 second bounds only apply when it's switched on. 0 is also allowed in the US profile. | The NHS call-for-advice wording has no duration. Switching the duration rule off only makes the app suggest calling sooner, never later. | Open |
| T2 | `patternWindowSec` is the hold before "Time to call". UK default is off (0): the NHS says to call when contractions are regular and every 5 minutes or more often, and gives no time. A midwife's hold can be set at 30 to 60 minutes, never longer. US stays at 1 hour (5-1-1). Changed 26 Oct 2026 (B14): the 1-hour UK hold showed "Keep timing" while the NHS call point was already met. | Turning the hold off only makes the app suggest calling sooner, like T1. **Midwife decision needed:** is "call at the NHS rate" right for first babies, or should the UK keep a short hold? | Open |
| T3 | `urgentSingleDurationSec` is 2 minutes and can be tightened to 90 seconds but never relaxed. | NHS: call urgently if any contraction lasts longer than 2 minutes. | Open |
| T4 | `urgentCountIn10Min` is 6 and can be tightened to 5. | NHS: call urgently at 6 or more in 10 minutes. NICE defines tachysystole as more than 5 in 10. | Open |
| T5 | `regularityCV` 0.35 (bounds 0.25 to 0.5) is app-defined. Since B14 it says how far over the advice interval the longer gaps may run for a pattern right at the limit to count as regular: median at or under 5 minutes and three in four at or under 6:45. It's measured against the limit, never the median, so a shorter gap can never fail it, and it can only let a pattern through, never hold back a frequent one. It still decides whether copy says "regular" or "uneven" (interquartile range over the median). | The old test (interquartile range over the median, both ways) kept contractions 2 to 5 minutes apart at "No regular pattern yet". | T6 | Early callers (second or later baby, previous fast labour, journey over 30 minutes) wait at most 30 minutes when a hold is set (a midwife's UK hold, or the US default). With the UK default of no hold (T2) it makes no difference. Not editable in v1. | NHS: call earlier for subsequent babies and long journeys, without a number. | Open |
| T7 | The US profile reuses the NHS urgent rules (over 2 minutes, 6 in 10). | ACOG publishes no numeric rule. | Open |

## Engine behaviour (GoNoGoEngine)

How the engine turns NHS wording into rules. The code is in `Sources/MissionCore/Engine/`; every rule below has a test, and the scripted labours in `Tests/MissionCoreTests/Fixtures/` show each one end to end.

| # | Rule | Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Frequency is start to start; duration is start to end. | Matches how NHS and ACOG describe timing. | Open |
| E2 | Statistics use the contractions in the last hour (fixed; no setting changes it), and need at least 4. The call rule is judged on the current rate: the contractions in the last 20 minutes, or the last 4 if there are fewer. | Fewer than 4 shows "not enough recent contractions". The current rate means a labour that speeds up is judged on how it is now, not on its slower start. | Open |
| E3 | Distances are measured in the run's pace: the upper quartile of its gaps in the hour before its last start, so two close contractions don't make the pace look short and it only changes when he taps. No contraction for 3× the pace: the app asks "Are they still coming?" and keeps the advice. No contraction for 5×: the pattern is stale and the 15-minute hold (E11) starts. A gap over 5× starts a new run. A gap within 0.2 of 2× to 5× the run's typical interval counts as missed taps only if the run is otherwise regular, the next gap is back on rhythm, and such gaps are at most a quarter of the run (so coupled contractions are never "missed taps"); it's said on the call as "possibly N not logged". Until the next contraction confirms the rhythm, the advice is held with the "paused" note. Starts more than a minute in the future are bad timestamps: left out of every statistic and flagged to fix. | B14. Three missed taps used to restart the hour and drop "Time to call". A slowdown, an ordinary longer gap or an irregular run is never filled in, so missing data can't make a pattern look quicker or more regular than it was. | Open |
| E4 | The call rate is met when any of these hold: at least three in four of the recent intervals (counted) are at or under the advice interval; their median is at or under it and three in four are at or under 1.35× it (T5); or, over at least 6 intervals of the run in the last hour, the average is at or under it and no gap is over twice it, which covers coupled contractions. The same tests at 1.5× give "Keep timing". Frequent but uneven contractions, or ones met on the average, read "Frequent, every 1 min to 7 min". | B14. Every test is one-sided: a property test checks that shortening any interval never fails the rule, another that a labour that only speeds up never gets later advice, and another that a looser threshold never gives later advice than a tighter one. Over a whole log, shortening one gap can still give later advice in three known ways: a hold isn't reached yet because time is compressed; an older, longer gap slides into the 20-minute window; or a run stops being regular enough for a missed-tap gap to be filled in. Coupled contractions were "Keep timing" for hours. **Midwife question:** is "on average every 5 minutes, no gap over 10, over 6 or more" right for coupled contractions? | Open |
| E5 | "Sustained" runs from the first contraction of the current run to the earlier of now and one typical interval of the run after the last start. It only matters when a hold is set (T2). It never shrinks without a new tap. | Silence doesn't count. A slower but unbroken lead-in counts towards the hour, which leans towards calling earlier (fixture 32). | Open |
| E6 | "6 or more in 10 minutes" counts starts in any half-open 10-minute window, so contractions every 2 minutes (5 per 10 minutes) don't trigger it, and every 100 seconds does. | Inclusive counting would flag every-2-minute contractions. | Open |
| E7 | A contraction over 2 minutes in the last hour means call now. | | Open |
| E8 | A contraction still being timed past 2 minutes also means call now, worded "If it's still going, call now. If you forgot to stop the timer, fix it." Past 5 minutes it's left out of duration statistics and flagged for editing. | Amendment B13.3. | Open |
| E9 | Under 37+0 weeks, any labour session means call now. Gestational age is fixed when labour mode starts. With no due date, the app asks "Is she 37 weeks or more?"; until he answers yes, it's call now ("We don't know how many weeks she is. If she could be under 37 weeks, call now."). | B14. Working it out at each evaluation flipped "call now" to "rest" at midnight on 37+0, and a missing due date switched the rule off silently. | Open |
| E10 | Red flags hold until the partner turns them off; turning one off takes effect at once. | Undo is the fix for a mis-tap. | Open |
| E11 | Contraction-driven advice rises immediately but only drops after 15 minutes of lower readings, showing "The pattern has slowed or paused. This advice stays up for 15 minutes in case it picks up again." It drops to the highest level seen in those 15 minutes. While new contractions are being logged but there are still too few to measure, the hold doesn't run down. | **Question for Phase 3:** corrections (editing, deleting or undoing a contraction) are also held for 15 minutes, so fixing a mis-tap that triggered "call now" keeps it up with the easing note. Safe, but possibly annoying. The old wording "Contractions have eased" was untrue in that case. | Open |
| E12 | When a hold is set, second or later babies, a previous fast labour, or a journey over 30 minutes need the pattern for 30 minutes at most (see T6). | | Open |
| E13 | "Keep timing" (establishing) also covers a regular pattern up to 1.5× the advice interval (7.5 minutes by default). | | Open |
| E14 | Engine copy is instructional ("Time to call your maternity unit"), never diagnostic. A test checks every line against "established labour", "active labour", "you are in labour" and "diagnos". | Sources shown under each line: NHS signs of labour; RCOG for the cord prolapse wording ("Don't touch it. Help her onto her knees with her bottom up, or onto her left side with her hips raised."). | Open |
| E15 | The US profile says "your provider or labor and delivery unit" and 911. | | Open |
| E16 | If the phone and watch both press start within 20 seconds with no stop between, it's one contraction. | | Open |
| E17 | "They've stopped" (an event in the log) drops non-urgent advice at once, with no 15-minute hold. If the next contraction comes on rhythm anyway (within 3× the pace of the last one), it was a mis-tap or a short pause and is cancelled. Otherwise the next contraction starts a new run, which needs 4 contractions and has to meet the call point again; the call-point time (E20) is kept until then, in case it was a mis-tap. It never clears an urgent rule (over 2 minutes, 6 in 10) or a red flag. A "they've stopped" stamped in the future is ignored. | B14. | Open |
| E18 | A tap on a contraction that has been open longer than 5 minutes asks "Did it just end, or is this a new one?" rather than guessing. "It just ended" records it, and a real contraction that long means call now for the hour. "This is a new one" starts the next contraction and leaves the old one flagged, with quick fixes of about 30, 60 or 90 seconds or its full length. A second tap within 1.5 seconds (either way, for clock skew between devices) is ignored as a double-tap, and a stop is never recorded before its start. A tap only ever stops the latest contraction; an earlier one without an end is a fix-later item, and a start stamped in the future is never "open". Shared by phone and watch. | B14. Always stopping it recorded a false 10-minute contraction; always starting a new one erased a real long one. | Open |
| E19 | Intervals are shown to the nearest 5 seconds ("every 5:20") and durations to the second, but never rounded across a rule: a gap over 5 minutes never reads "5 min", a 59.6-second contraction under a 60-second rule never reads "60 sec", and a 120.4-second one reads "121 sec", flagged, on the triage card. | B14. "About every 5 min. Not at the call point yet." contradicted the rule it quoted. | Open |
| E20 | Once the contraction pattern has met the call point in a session, a gap in the log doesn't make it hold again, unless he has since said they stopped. Only the pattern sets this, never an urgent rule or a red flag. The time is on the triage card ("Call point: reached at 14:05"). After a real "they've stopped" it resets, and the next run has to meet the call point again. | B14. | Open |
| E21 | The engine re-clamps every threshold to its safe bounds on each run, so a stored, synced or corrupt profile can't relax a safety rule, and the output carries the clamped thresholds for all copy and alerts. The urgent rules always look back a full hour. The threshold editor can tell which changes delay advice (`ThresholdTable.delaysAdvice`) and should label them "only if your midwife told you". | B14. | Open |
| E22 | While taps resume after a gap but the new run is too short to measure, the advice is kept and its 15-minute dwell restarts, so it gets its full quiet and dwell time when the taps stop again. It only starts on a new tap within an hour of the one before, so a correction, a delete or a tap after a night's sleep never holds advice up. | B14. | Open |
| E23 | The next review time includes the instant after every moment a contraction leaves the 20-minute rate window or the hour, and an open contraction reaching a duration rule, so a rise that comes from time passing is scheduled on time. A property test checks the planner never schedules a rise later than a 5-second walk finds it. | B14. | Open |

## Calling (B14)

| # | Rule | Notes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | Saved numbers are checked when they're entered, not when they're dialled: UK formats ("0113 392 6731", "+44 (0)113…", "0044…", brackets, any dash, dots, "Tel:", and the invisible marks iOS adds when copying from Contacts) are stored as E.164; 999, 111, 112 and 116 123 dial as they are. "Option 2", "(option 2)", "ext 4" and "*2#" become a note ("Then press 2") because iOS won't dial * or #. Rejected: letters, two numbers in one field (including one hidden after "option"), UK numbers without their leading 0 or +44 (ten digits without a 0 are probably a US number), UK numbers starting 04 or 06, UK numbers with 9 digits after the 0 except some 0800 numbers and areas with five-digit local numbers (01xxx codes other than the big-city 011x and 01x1), US numbers that aren't valid NANP, a US "011" call without a full-length number, and overseas numbers unless the field allows them. A bracketed 0 after any country code is dropped. A saved number is parsed again when it's read back; if it doesn't come back the same it won't decode, and it never makes a tel: link. | The old digit filter turned "+44 (0)113 392 6731" into +4401133926731 and "option 2" into an extra digit. | Open |
| K2 | "Can't get through?": try again, the labour ward or switchboard if saved, NHS 111 (UK), then 999 or 911 if it's an emergency. | NHS: call 111 if you can't reach your midwife or maternity unit (signs that labour has begun). | Open |
| K3 | MissionCore marks labour alerts that ask him to call, and the "still going?" nudge, as time sensitive, and gives each one an identifier that's the same on every device: `labour.<session>.rise.<state>` for a rise whether posted at once or scheduled, `labour.<session>.reason.<reason>` for a new urgent reason, `labour.<session>.stillGoing` for the nudge. A new urgent contraction reason (over 2 minutes, 6 in 10) alerts even when "call now" is already showing for another reason. One long contraction is one alert: the nudge and both over-2-minutes reasons share `labour.<session>.overUrgentDuration`, and stopping it doesn't alert again. Nothing asks him to call the unit while 999 is showing: no nudge, and no new-reason alert. | **App work, not done yet:** add the Time Sensitive Notifications capability to the app and watch targets and set the level; before each new plan, remove pending requests under `labour.<session>.`; cancel only that prefix when a session ends. | Open |
